Schools

Teacher Training

I train teachers and school staff so they can understand how children’s behaviour is directly linked to their psychological experiences.

Using recent developments in neuroscience as well as psychological theories, schools can ensure that mental health and wellbeing are top of their agenda which in turn helps children to be able to learn well and be happy!

Helping teachers develop strategies and systems that mean all the children in the class have their psychological needs met whilst also creating an atmosphere where learning and the enjoyment of learning is top of the agenda!

Some of the trainings that schools have requested for their staff:

  • Neuroscience - Understanding how the brain affects learning
  • Classroom management - Inspiring good behaviour without resorting to shame
  • Emotional Support - creating a school with wellbeing at the centre
Emotional Literacy  -  Inspiring good behaviour  -  Neuroscience  -  Motivation Strategies  -  Well-Being Plans  -  Emotional Literacy  -  Inspiring good behaviour

Parenting Workshops

In schools

All workshops can be tailored to meet the needs of the parents at your school.

Topics can include:

  • Independence and Resilience
  • Marvellous Mornings, Brilliant Bedtimes
  • Happy Homework !
  • Connection for Co-operation
  • The Brain; Helping it to Help your Child
  • Growing strong Emotional Bonds

By learning new ways of communicating and building healthy strategies, parents can create a family life that involves lots of fun and laughter as well as children with strong self-esteem and lots of self-confidence!
